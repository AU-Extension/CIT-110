Slow computer diagnosis challenge

Ten help desk tickets from South Florida offices. Each one shows real-looking output from Task Manager, top, free, or df. Find the cause, then pick the fix a careful technician would use.

How to use this interactive
  1. Read the ticket and study the evidence. Look for the number that stands out.
  2. Choose the most likely cause. You get one try, worth 5 points.
  3. Choose the best fix. One try, worth 5 points.
  4. Read the explanation, then go to the next ticket. Your score out of 100 appears at the end.
