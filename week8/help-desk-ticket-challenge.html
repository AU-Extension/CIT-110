Help desk ticket challenge

Your first shift at a help desk that supports small businesses across Miami-Dade. First, put the six troubleshooting steps in order. Then work nine tickets: choose the next best step and the right tool for each one.

How to use this challenge
  1. Part 1 (10 points): use the arrow buttons to put the six troubleshooting steps in the correct order, then press Check order.
  2. Part 2 (90 points): for each ticket, answer two questions. The next best step is worth 5 points and the right tool is worth 5 points.
  3. Read the explanation after every answer. It names the troubleshooting step and the tool a technician would really use.
  4. Your final score and a summary of every ticket appear at the end.
