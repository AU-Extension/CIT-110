Troubleshooting methodology flowchart

Help desk technicians follow the same six steps for every problem, from a blue screen to a dead network connection. This is the troubleshooting method used in CompTIA A+. Walk through two real-style tickets, make the call at each step, and watch the ticket notes fill in.

How to use this interactive
  1. Choose a ticket: a Windows blue screen or an Ubuntu computer with no network.
  2. At each step, read the evidence and choose what you would do. The explanation appears right after.
  3. See which tools the technician uses and what gets written in the ticket notes on the right.
  4. Move to the next step, or click any finished step in the flowchart to review it.
  5. Your good calls are counted for your own practice. Nothing here is graded.
    Loop: if the test in step 3 does not confirm your theory, go back to step 2 with a new theory, or escalate the ticket to someone with more experience.
