OS layers explorer

An operating system sits between you and the hardware. Explore each layer, see its Windows and Linux names, and follow everyday actions as they travel down to the hardware and back.

How to use this interactive
  1. Use the Windows 11 and Ubuntu Linux switch to see the real names of each layer in both systems.
  2. Click any layer in the stack to read what it does and when you notice it.
  3. In "Follow a click", pick an action and press Next step (or Play all) to watch which layer does the work.
  4. Answer the six questions at the bottom to check your understanding.
