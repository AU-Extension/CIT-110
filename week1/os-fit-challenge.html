Which OS fits?

Ten real-world cases from around South Florida. For each one, choose the operating system that fits best, then the key reason. The deciding factor is usually the software people need or the hardware they have.

How to use this interactive
  1. Read who the user is and what they need.
  2. Step 1: choose the operating system that fits best (5 points).
  3. Step 2: choose the key reason for that choice (5 points).
  4. Read the explanation, then move to the next case. Your final score appears after case 10.
