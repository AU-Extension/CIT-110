Hardening checklist challenge

You are the IT technician for Miracle Mile Realty in Coral Gables. The front desk laptop holds client contracts and goes home with the receptionist. Review its security settings, find the risky ones, and fix them.

How to use this interactive
  1. Part 1: mark each of the 12 settings as Risk or OK, then press Check my review (3 points per setting).
  2. Part 2: for each real risk, choose the best fix. One try per risk, 8 points each.
  3. Read the explanation after every fix. Some wrong answers make the laptop less safe.
  4. At the end, see your score out of 100 and the order a technician would fix these risks in.
